EVIDENCE WORKSPACE

How the beta works and what is not automated

The beta retrieves up to 60 recent SEC filings from the past 90 days. Metadata categories are tentative; they do not verify a positive or negative investment thesis. Form 4 does not necessarily mean an insider bought shares.

Your confirmed assessments contribute once per category for 30 days from the filing acceptance time. Combined score = 75% of the existing technical score + your catalyst adjustment (−25 to +25), bounded 0–100. Missing/stale data suppresses the combined score.

A candidate needs: beta score ≥75, positive assessed evidence, price above both 50/200-day averages, valid stop < entry < target, target-2 risk/reward ≥2 and report price within 3% of entry. These are unvalidated screening rules, not trade instructions. Upcoming earnings are not automatically known.

SEC cache: one hour. Stale evidence: 24 hours. Technical report freshness: maximum four calendar days, including weekends; this conservative age check is not an exchange-calendar guarantee. Refreshing filings does not refresh prices.

Reuters, Bloomberg, FactSet and IBKR are external research links only. No paid reports are copied. Your notes are private, source filings are shared with approved users. Production rankings and trading remain unchanged.
