Portfolio analysis and human review

Connect this page to a deployed PIP API to import portfolio files, optionally sync a server-configured IBKR Flex Query, load available Yahoo research and shared market prices, persist portfolio analysis, rank holdings against your required return, and review auditable recommendations. Market facts are displayed with their sources; PIP does not invent score conversions for raw returns.

IBKR sync is read-only; order transmission is disabled. Live Flex sync works only after the API, Supabase Vault migration, Edge Function, and server-only shared secret are configured. Approval records a human decision; it does not submit an order.

To see your saved holdings and run each analysis module one at a time, open the PIP module runner. It only reads from the API; use this workbench to import, sync, and approve.

Portfolio review · read-only

Ranks the open positions in the selected portfolio by how far each one trails the annual return you require, then shows dead-money and replacement advice for the furthest-behind few. It reads saved data only. It does not run when you import or sync a portfolio, and it never places or transmits orders.

Returns here are price-only, because dividends received are not part of imported positions. Dividend payers can therefore look further behind than they are, and a dead-money verdict is withheld until you analyze that holding with dividends entered. Only holdings kept for at least a year are ranked. Replacements are compared only when you supply candidate scores, and only against the holding's saved CPS. For catch-up or replacement timing scenarios, analyze one holding under Score formula review.
