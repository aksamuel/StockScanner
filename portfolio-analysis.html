CSV format and import details

Required columns: symbol, quantity, and buy_price. Buy date and currency may be omitted; choose a currency for rows without one. Native IBKR exports using Symbol, Quantity, CostBasisPrice, and OpenDateTime are also accepted, including YYYYMMDD dates.

How the analysis works

The page combines present profit/loss, days held, the newest daily StockScanner recommendation and score, targets and stop-losses, and each symbol's share of known portfolio market value.

Recovery scenario also shows the percentage-point gap to the compounded annual target and estimated additional market days based on the arithmetic average daily adjusted-close growth.

The annual target (7% by default) compounds gradually across the selected review window at 252 market days per year and scales with the latest current price.

Concentration thresholds: 10% warrants closer review and 20% is treated as high concentration. Decisions are screening prompts for your review, not automatic orders or guarantees.

IBKR may omit buy dates unless its Flex query uses Open Positions at tax-lot detail. Missing dates are never guessed.

Technical strength appears once inside Action review: below 40 is Weak (red), 40–70 is Moderate (orange), and above 70 is Strong (green). These describe technical condition rather than a prediction of future performance.
