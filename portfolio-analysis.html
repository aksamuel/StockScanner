How the analysis works

The page combines present profit/loss, days held, the newest daily StockScanner recommendation and score, targets and stop-losses, and each symbol's share of known portfolio market value. A manual target remains the highest-priority target. Otherwise, the automatic target is the lowest valid price at or above cost from the 7% return objective, Technical Target 1, resistance and the analyst target proxy. A reached target or stop takes priority. An Avoid rating combined with a loss of at least 10% produces a sell review; high concentration, weaker trend, long holding time, or a gain of at least 7% can produce a partial-sell review. Missing evidence is disclosed and never guessed.

Concentration thresholds: 10% warrants closer review and 20% is treated as high concentration. Decisions are screening prompts for your review, not automatic orders or guaranteed outcomes.

IBKR may omit buy dates unless its Flex query uses Open Positions at tax-lot detail. Missing dates are never guessed.

Technical strength appears once inside Action review: below 40 is Weak (red), 40–70 is Moderate (orange), and above 70 is Strong (green). These describe technical conditions, not buy/sell instructions. Recovery and Profit / Loss % are beside Action review.

View recovery graph compares the stock's own historical growth rate with the Equal-weight Top 20 rate, starting at the latest available price today and aiming at your buy price. Both estimated breakeven dates remain visible. They are scenarios, not guaranteed dates, and exclude fees, taxes, dividends received, and currency conversion. Historical recovery statistics inside the graph show comparable past declines, including unrecovered episodes. Recovery scenarios do not change the action-review signal.
