How the analysis works

The page compares buy price with the newest available present price. A target or stop entered in the CSV takes priority. Otherwise, +20% produces a profit-review signal and −10% produces a risk-review signal. These are transparent screening rules, not automatic trade instructions or personalized financial advice.

IBKR may omit buy dates unless its Flex query uses Open Positions at tax-lot detail. Missing dates are never guessed.
