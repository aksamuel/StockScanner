How this first version reviews holdings

Prices are selected per symbol from the newer of the latest hourly snapshot and the latest daily scan, using their timestamps. The review reuses StockScanner's existing portfolio-analysis rules: target or stop events, the 7% profit-review threshold, the -10% risk threshold when the scan says Avoid, and the existing concentration and holding-period checks. These are review prompts, not trade instructions. Currency must be explicitly available or selected before return-based rules run; the scan price is treated as USD.

The CSV itself is read locally. The page makes read-only requests for StockScanner scan prices and daily scan evidence. It does not upload holdings, save changes, or submit orders. A short lot is shown as short exposure; mixed long and short lots for one symbol require manual review.
